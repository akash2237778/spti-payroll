Shifts & working-hour rules

Worked time = last punch − first punch − unpaid lunch. With a fixed lunch window (e.g. 13:00 for 30 min) lunch is deducted only for the part of the window the person was present: 09:00–17:30 = 8 h, 14:00–20:00 = 6 h (no lunch taken). Without a window, the break is deducted once the span is longer than "deduct break after". Employees marked Paid lunch break (on their profile) never lose it. OT = worked time beyond the shift's standard hours (shift length − break), counted only above the OT threshold and rounded down to the OT block. Punches before the "day starts at" hour count towards the previous day, so late check-outs after midnight are handled.
