Attendance rules

Short days (by hours worked)

Uses each shift's full day and half day hours (Shifts page; General: full day from 6 h, half day from 4 h, standard 8 h): 6 h – 8 h worked = full day, the hours short of 8 h are taken off that month's overtime (never below zero, never from salary, not for hourly staff); 4 h – 6 h = half day, the time worked beyond 4 h is paid as overtime; under 4 h = absent. Example: 09:00–16:00 (6:30) = full day, 1:30 less OT; 09:00–14:00 (4:30) = half day + 0:30 OT.

Late-comer penalty — suggested minutes

The penalty is chosen day by day on each employee's sheet (Attendance → employee → "Late penalty" column). Only days you penalize reduce that employee's overtime; OT never goes below zero and salary is never cut. These two numbers only pre-fill the suggested minutes. Example: 30 + 1 × minutes late → 40 min late suggests 70 min.

Salary rules

Statutory

ESI (ESIC): wages u/s 2(88) of the Code on Social Security, 2020 (applied by ESIC from 21 Nov 2025): gross pay minus overtime allowance, with excluded items above half of total pay added back. Covered if ESI wages are within the ceiling, decided per contribution period (Apr–Sep, Oct–Mar); once covered, contributions continue to the end of the period. Each share is rounded up to the next rupee; no employee share when the average daily wage is at or below the low-wage limit (the employer still pays). An establishment with 10 or more employees must cover every eligible employee.

Only employees with ESI ticked on their profile. Unticked = no ESI deducted.
